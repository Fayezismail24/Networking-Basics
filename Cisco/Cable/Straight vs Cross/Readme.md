**Computers transmit on pins 1 & 2 and receive on pins 3 & 6**

**Switches do the opposite — transmit on pins 3 & 6 and receive on pins 1 & 2.**

**Since their TX/RX pins naturally complement each other, a straight-through cable works fine between them.**

**However  when connecting PC to PC, both devices transmit on the same pins (1 & 2), so a crossover cable is required to rewire TX on one end to RX on the other.**
