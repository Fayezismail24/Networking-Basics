## Ethernet Ports Overview

All five ports on this device are **10/100/1000 Mbps Gigabit Ethernet** ports, providing high-speed wired connectivity.

---

## Port 1 (Internet / PoE-in)

**Internet (WAN):**  
- By default, **Port 1** is configured as the **WAN (Internet) port**.  
- This is where you connect the Ethernet cable coming from your **ISP modem** or upstream router.

**PoE-in (Passive Power over Ethernet):**  
- Port 1 also supports **Passive PoE-in**.  
- You can power the entire router through this port using a **PoE injector**.  
- When using PoE-in, the **DC power jack is not required**.

⚠️ Note: Ensure the PoE voltage matches the device specifications to avoid damage.

---

## Ports 2, 3, 4, and 5 (LAN)

**Local Area Network (LAN):**  
- These ports are used to connect **internal network devices**, such as:
  - Desktop or laptop computers  
  - Wi-Fi Access Points  
  - Network switches  
  - IP phones or printers

**Default Behavior:**  
- In the default configuration, **Ports 2–5 are bridged together**, acting as a **built-in switch**.  
- Devices connected to these ports are on the **same LAN subnet** and can communicate directly with each other.

---

## Summary

- **Port 1:** WAN + Passive PoE-in  
- **Ports 2–5:** LAN switch ports  
- **Speed:** Gigabit Ethernet on all ports  
- **Power Options:** DC jack or Passive PoE-in
