# MikroTik Overview

## Introduction to MikroTik

**MikroTik** is a Latvian company that produces network equipment and software solutions. The company's **RouterOS** is one of the most popular and powerful operating systems for routers, providing users with comprehensive networking features like routing, firewall, bandwidth management, and more.

MikroTik products are widely used in ISPs (Internet Service Providers), small businesses, and home networks due to their affordability and robust capabilities.

---

## MikroTik Hardware

MikroTik produces a variety of **networking devices** like:

1. **RouterBoard**: These are specialized devices running RouterOS, including models like hAP (home access point), CCR (Cloud Core Router), hEX (small, cost-effective router), and more.
2. **Cloud Core Routers (CCR)**: High-performance devices designed for larger networks or ISP-level deployments.
3. **RB (RouterBoard)**: Small, cost-effective devices aimed at SOHO (small office/home office) and SOHO ISPs.
4. **Wireless Systems**: Including **access points**, **repeaters**, and **CPE (Customer Premise Equipment)** devices.

### Types of MikroTik Routers:

#### **1. hEX Series** (Small to Medium Home/Office Routers)
- The **hEX** routers are affordable, compact, and provide high-performance Ethernet routing. These devices focus on **Ethernet-only** networking and do not include Wi-Fi capabilities but can be used with separate access points for wireless connectivity.
  - **hEX** (RB750Gr3) – 5 Gigabit Ethernet ports, 1 GHz CPU, no Wi-Fi.
  - **hEX POE** (RB960PGS) – 5 Gigabit Ethernet ports, PoE support on all ports, 1 GHz CPU.
  - **hEX S** (RB760iGS) – 5 Gigabit Ethernet ports, compact form, no PoE support.

#### **2. hAP Series** (Home Access Points)
- The **hAP** series routers are aimed at home users and small offices, offering both **Ethernet** and **Wi-Fi** connectivity. They combine the benefits of wired and wireless networking in a single device.
  - **hAP Lite** – 5 Ethernet ports, 1 USB port, Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz).
  - **hAP ac** – 5 Gigabit Ethernet ports, Dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz).
  - **hAP ac²** – 5 Gigabit Ethernet ports, Dual-band Wi-Fi with improved performance.

#### **3. RB Series** (Small RouterBoards)
- The **RB** series are small, cost-effective routers suitable for small office/home office setups. These models usually include Ethernet ports for wired networking.
  - **RB2011** – Available in multiple variants with 5 to 10 Ethernet ports and an SFP port for fiber connectivity.
  - **RB1100AHx4** – 10 Gigabit Ethernet ports, used for higher traffic demands.

#### **4. Cloud Core Routers (CCR)** (High-Performance Enterprise/ISP Routers)
- The **CCR** routers are designed for high-performance routing, ideal for **ISPs**, **data centers**, or large-scale enterprise networks. These devices offer multiple Gigabit or even 10-Gigabit Ethernet ports, advanced routing protocols, and support for **fiber** and **high-speed connections**.
  - **CCR1009-7G-1C-1S+** – 7 Gigabit Ethernet ports, 1 SFP port, 1 Combo port.
  - **CCR1036-8G-2S+** – 8 Gigabit Ethernet ports, 2 SFP+ ports (10 Gbps fiber).
  - **CCR1072-1G-8S+** – 1 Gigabit Ethernet port, 8 SFP+ ports, designed for very high throughput.

#### **5. SXT Series** (Outdoor Wi-Fi Solutions)
- **SXT** models are designed for outdoor use, often in point-to-point (PtP) and point-to-multipoint (PtMP) wireless connections. They are used for **wireless bridging** and **long-distance connections**.
  - **SXT Lite5** – 1 Gigabit Ethernet port, Wi-Fi (5 GHz), ideal for long-range wireless links.
  - **SXT SA5** – 1 Gigabit Ethernet port, Wi-Fi (5 GHz), high-gain antenna for longer distance communication.

#### **6. wAP Series** (Wireless Access Points)
- The **wAP** models are small and compact devices primarily used for providing **Wi-Fi coverage** in outdoor or hard-to-reach areas.
  - **wAP 60G** – 1 Gigabit Ethernet port, 60 GHz Wi-Fi for high-speed outdoor links.
  - **wAP LTE** – 1 Gigabit Ethernet port, 1 LTE modem port for mobile data connectivity.

---

## Conclusion

MikroTik offers a broad range of routers catering to different needs, from **small home networks** to **large-scale enterprise** and **ISP deployments**. The **hEX** series is perfect for budget-conscious users seeking powerful routing without wireless capabilities, while **Cloud Core Routers (CCR)** serve those needing high-performance, enterprise-level networking. Whether you're setting up a **home network**, **small office**, or managing an **ISP**, MikroTik provides affordable, reliable solutions across its diverse product line.
