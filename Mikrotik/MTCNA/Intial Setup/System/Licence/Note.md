
# MikroTik RouterOS License User Connection Limits

### **License Level 4**:
- **Hotspot Server**: Supports **up to 200 users** connecting to your Hotspot server.
- **PPPoe Server**: Supports **up to 200 users** connecting to your PPPoE server.
- **Tunnels (e.g., VPN)**: Supports **up to 200 users** connecting to your Tunnel service (e.g., L2TP, PPTP, etc.).

### **License Level 5**:
- **Hotspot Server**: Supports **up to 500 users** connecting to your Hotspot server.
- **PPPoe Server**: Supports **up to 500 users** connecting to your PPPoE server.
- - **Tunnels (e.g., VPN)**: Supports **up to 500 users** connecting to your Tunnel service (e.g., L2TP, PPTP, etc.).


### **License Level 6**:
- **Hotspot Server**: Supports **unlimited users** connecting to your Hotspot server.
- **PPPoe Server**: Supports **unlimited users** connecting to your PPPoE server.
- **Tunnels (e.g., VPN)**: Supports **unlimited users** connecting to your Tunnel service (e.g., L2TP, PPTP, etc.).


---

### Key Takeaways:
- **Level 4**: Supports **up to 200 users** for Hotspot, PPPoE, and Tunnel connections, suitable for small to medium-sized deployments.
- **Level 5**: Supports **up to 500 users**, making it ideal for larger networks.
- **Level 6**: Offers **unlimited users**, perfect for enterprise-level or very large networks.
